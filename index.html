PRIVATE LETTER07 · 10 · 26

DEAR VAISHNAVI,

If I could give you
one thing today...

It would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes, even for a moment. Maybe then you'd understand how much warmth, beauty and happiness you bring into my life.

I still think about 28 April — Starbucks, our first meeting, and that strange little feeling that something important had just begun. I didn't know then how many memories would follow.

On your birthday, I don't just wish you success or happiness. I wish you the kind of days that make you stop and smile for no reason, dreams that come true slowly and beautifully, and a life filled with people who love you honestly.

Thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my story. And if I get to choose, I want to keep writing the next chapters with you.

Forever yours,
Durgesh ♥
